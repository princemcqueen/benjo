RIDE A DRAGON - ADMIN /GIVE SYSTEM
==================================

The system is already embedded in RideADragon_Dev.rbxlx.
It is SERVER-SIDE and uses the existing game services/DataStore.
A successful grant requests an immediate save.

LIVE ADMIN ACCESS
  ReplicatedStorage > Configs > AdminConfig

  Current username allowed:
    Bendzaminoo

  For extra admins, add their numeric Roblox UserId to AdminUserIds.
  In Studio, everyone is allowed so the commands are easy to test.

COMMANDS
  /give me Cash 10000
  /give me Spins 25
  /give PlayerName PrinceDragon
  /give PlayerName PrinceDragon 2
  /give PlayerName LuckPotion 5
  /give PlayerName CelestialEgg 3
  /give PlayerName EggHunter
  /givehelp

PLAYER TARGET
  Use a full username, a unique username/display-name prefix, or "me".

WHAT CAN BE GIVEN
  - Cash / Spins
  - Every dragon species in DragonConfig, including OwnerDragon, PrinceDragon,
    N2RC1SDragon and M0nicaDragon
  - Every egg in EggConfig
  - Every Developer Product in MonetizationConfig.Products
  - Permanent Game Pass perk flags in MonetizationConfig.GamePasses (e.g. EggHunter)

POTIONS
  Potions in this game are immediate timed boosts, not bag items.
  Example: /give Player LuckPotion 5 applies five normal Luck Potions and therefore
  extends the Luck timer by 5 x 15 minutes.

SECURITY
  - Permission is checked on the server.
  - Grants use EconomyService, SpinService, DragonService, EggService and
    MonetizationService directly.
  - No client RemoteEvent can choose arbitrary amounts.
  - Per-command limits are in AdminConfig.

RANKS (added in TEST 9)
-----------------------
  /rank <player|me> <rank|none>     rank = vip, mod, admin, coowner, owner (or the numbers 1-5)
  /ranks                            the ranks and who has one in this server
  /rankhelp

  Examples:
    /rank Bob vip
    /rank Bob co-owner
    /rank me owner
    /rank Bob none
    /rank SomeUsername vip           (not in the server: they get it when they join)

  Titles, epithets and colours: ReplicatedStorage > Configs > RankConfig.
  Who may: the owner (AdminConfig / the experience creator / anybody inside Studio) can set any rank.
  A CO-OWNER can hand out VIP, MOD and ADMIN to players below CO-OWNER and may also use /give.
  A rank is a tag over the character and a coloured [TITLE] in chat; it is saved with the player.
